Sync rules

Each direction between two calendars has its own rule. Work to Personal can copy titles and details, while Personal to Work shows only Busy.

Where to find them

On the Sync tab, What goes where lists every direction, such as School → Work, with its rule in a few words and how it went last time: Synced, Syncing, Error or Off. Press Edit to change one, or Resync to rewrite every copy going that way now.

On or off

Untick Copy events from A to B to stop that direction. You choose what happens to the copies already made:

  • Remove them at the next sync. The calendar is as if that direction never synced.
  • Keep them where they are. CalSync stops updating or deleting them. They stay until you delete them.

Stopping a whole calendar asks the same question about its copies on your other calendars.

Info to copy

Title
Off, and copies say the text you choose instead, such as Busy or Personal.
Location
The place, as written on the original.
Description
The original's notes, below CalSync's own line.
Video call link
A Meet, Teams or Zoom link from the original, added to the notes as Join.
Guest list
The guests' addresses, as text in the notes. Nobody is invited.
Reminders
On: the copy uses the calendar's usual reminder. Off by default, so you aren't alerted twice.
Mark copies private
Others who can see that calendar see only Busy.

A private event never shares its title, place or notes, whatever is ticked.

Tag and colour

A tag goes in front of each copy's title, such as [Work] Standup. The page shows how copies will look as you type. The colour can be the calendar's own, the same as the original, or one you pick. Google calendars use event colours; Microsoft calendars get a category with that name; CalDAV calendars get a colour where the app shows one.

Leave out

  • Events marked free, invites you answered Maybe, and invites you haven't answered.
  • All-day events, birthdays and out of office. Birthdays are left out unless you untick them.
  • Titles with certain words, or only titles with certain words.
  • Certain colours or categories, or only certain ones.
  • Only some days of the week, and only events that start between two times. All-day events skip the time check.

When a rule starts leaving an event out, its copy is removed at the next sync.

How far it syncs

In Settings, choose how many days back and ahead to sync. The default is 0 back and 120 ahead. Copies outside the window are left as they are.

Why is or isn't this copied?

Press Preview changes. Below the counts, every event is listed with where it would go. Open one to see the rule that decided, for example not copied, because its title has "lunch" or copied as "Busy".

Activity

The Activity page lists every sync, newest first: when, why it ran, and what it added, updated and removed. The last 300 runs are kept on your computer only.

Settings from before rules

Busy only and Don't copy X here still work. A rule you save for a direction takes over from them for that direction.
