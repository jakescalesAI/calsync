Booking apps

Calendly, Cal.com and other booking apps work with calsync. There is nothing to install and no key to paste.

Plug one in

  1. Add your calendars to calsync and start syncing.
  2. In your booking app, connect one of those calendars.
  3. Let the app check that calendar for conflicts, and send new bookings to it.

Pick a calendar calsync can write to. A published .ics link won't work here, because copies can't be written to it.

What happens next

Your busy times
A meeting on any calendar is copied to the one your booking app checks, so that time stops being offered.
New bookings
A booking lands on the connected calendar, and calsync copies it to the others on its next sync.
Free time
A copy keeps its busy or free status. An event marked free stays free, so it won't block a booking.
Private details
If that calendar's option Copies here only say Busy is on, the booking app sees Busy and no title.

Which calendars each app takes

Calendly
Google Calendar, Office 365, Outlook and Exchange.
Cal.com
Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar and CalDAV.
Anything else
Any booking app that connects to a calendar calsync syncs works the same way.

One thing to know

Copies are made on each sync, every 5 minutes unless you change it. A slot can still be booked in the minutes before a new meeting reaches that calendar. You can shorten that with How often to sync in Settings.
